# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web (mobile-first PWA-style web app; Android Chrome on cheap phones is the main device)

## Users
- **Kirana owner (primary):** runs a small neighbourhood grocery shop in Indore (tier 1/2 India). Often 40+, comfortable with WhatsApp and UPI, not with apps or typing. Uses the phone at the counter, standing, in a bright and noisy shop, often with one hand while serving customers. Job: make bills, track stock and udhaar (credit), close the day's accounts.
- **Customer of the shop:** opens the shop's page from a QR or WhatsApp link to order for pickup, see a bill, or see their udhaar balance.
- **Admin (the founder only):** creates stores, resets PINs, watches usage, handles bag requests.

## Product Purpose
OmniOS Dukaan is a voice-native shop app: the owner speaks ("Sharma ji, 2 kilo cheeni, 1 Maggi"), reviews a card, taps ✓, and the bill, stock and udhaar khata update. Success: an owner uses it daily without help, and the day's hisaab is right.

## Positioning
Voice first, in Hindi/Hinglish/English, built for loose items, udhaar and the kirana's own words — not a POS ported from supermarkets. Every voice action is dictate → review → ✓ before anything saves.

## Operating Context
Counter in a busy shop; interruptions; poor or patchy 4G; low-end Android; sunlight glare; customers watching. Bills shared as WhatsApp links. Supplier parchis (paper bills) for stock in. Mesa Future Founders Challenge: Phase 1 live by 3 Dec 2026.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- 4 tabs (Ghar/Home, Bill, Maal/Stock, Udhaar) + one mic on every screen; anything reachable in ≤ 2 taps from Home.
- Text ≥ 18px, buttons ≥ 56px, icon + word (never icon-only).
- Three languages everywhere: Hindi, Hinglish, English.
- Nothing fake in the app: real numbers or an honest "learning"/"coming soon" state.
- ₹0 spend; Gemini only when rules are unsure; works without it.
- Owner tests: every screen must score ≥ 9/10 for ease and calm with simulated owners.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: OmniOS Dukaan. Voice: warm, plain, respectful Hinglish ("Ho gaya", "Peeche", "Bolo"). Udhaar is not an error (never red for it).
