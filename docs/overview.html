Who

2 · The problem

The counter is the busiest place in India.

Kirana stores sell most of India's groceries, yet most still run on memory, a notebook and a calculator. Existing apps were built for people who type.

Typing doesn't fit the counter
One hand gives change, the other holds a phone. Searching menus for "Maggi 70g" while a queue waits doesn't happen.
Udhaar is the biggest risk
Credit lives in a diary. Balances are forgotten, reminders are awkward, money leaks.
Stock is a guess
Items run out without warning; supplier dues are tracked on paper slips.
Apps speak English
Owners think in Hinglish and Hindi; most tools are menus, forms and English labels.
3 · The product

Speak. That's the interface.

One teal Bolo bar sits in the same place on every screen. Everything else is a large, clear tap.

PrincipleWhat it means in the app

5 · Core features

Everything a kirana runs on.

Voice billing

  • Understands Hinglish, Hindi and English, including mis-hearings.
  • Quantities like "aadha kilo", "dedh", "do packet".
  • Customer recognised from the sentence; walk-ins supported.
  • Undo any bill; bill by tapping items when voice isn't possible.

Udhaar khata

  • Every customer's balance and history.
  • "Gupta ji ne 500 diye" records a payment by voice.
  • One-tap WhatsApp reminder and a public khata link.
  • Biggest dues surfaced on the home screen.

Stock (Maal)

  • "Maal aaya, 10 Aashirvaad atta" adds stock.
  • Photo of a supplier bill → stock in + supplier dues.
  • "Cheeni kitni bachi?" answered aloud.
  • Daily 3-item count with a streak keeps stock honest.

Hisaab & galla

  • Sales by day, week, month; Cash / UPI / Udhaar split.
  • "Galla mein 4,200 hai" checks the cash drawer against bills.
  • Morning briefing with what needs attention.

Customer side

  • Professional e-bill with letterhead, flavour and size per line.
  • Shop's own online store and order tracking.

Admin dashboard

  • Create, pause and manage stores; reset PINs.
  • Phones logged in, requests, demand — in one place.
UPI — QR only, amount inside
Saved — khata ticket, WhatsApp, next bill

6 · The Universe & flavour engine

It already knows the shelf.

A shop doesn't set up a catalogue. OmniOS ships with the products Indian kirana stores actually sell — in the words owners actually use.

1,500products across staples, snacks, dairy, drinks, personal care, cleaning, pooja, baby, pet and more
5,226flavours and pack sizes — Maggi Masala 70g, Coca-Cola 750ml, Aashirvaad 5kg
3names per item — Hindi, Hinglish, English — plus spoken mis-hearings

Flavour engine — smart without interrupting

SituationWhat OmniOS does
Owner says "Maggi"Picks the shop's usual flavour, else the default (Masala 70g). A small "badlo" lets them change it.
Owner says "Maggi chicken"Uses exactly that — no question.
Shop sells a real mix of flavoursShows the top 2–3 as one-tap chips, usual one pre-ticked. Billing never stops.

Prices belong to the shop

Each shop keeps its own price: from stock entry or set by hand, otherwise learned automatically after three bills at the same price. The owner can always change it.

Fast and offline-friendly

The whole Universe loads once (~114 KB), stays on the phone and matches a spoken word in about 4 milliseconds.

Positioning

How each competitor wins today

  • Khatabook, OkCredit — simple digital udhaar ledgers with reminders; light on billing and stock.
  • Vyapar, myBillBook — GST invoicing and accounting depth; built for typing at a desk.
  • Payment apps' merchant tools — great for collecting UPI; not a shop operating system.
  • Paper diary — free and instant; no totals, reminders or stock.

Technology

LayerApproach
AppInstallable web app (PWA) — React, TypeScript, Vite; hosted on Vercel. No app-store install needed.
VoicePhone speech-to-text → fast rule-based Hinglish parser on the phone → AI fallback only when unsure.
DataSupabase (Postgres). Row-level security: every shop can see only its own data.
MoneyAll bills, payments and stock changes run as single server transactions — no half-saved bills.
AccessShop code + PIN; lock after 5 wrong tries; see and remove logged-in phones.
Quality85 automated tests; indexed database; universe matching benchmarked per release.
